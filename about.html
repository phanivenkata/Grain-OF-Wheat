About us

Growing together in Christ.

Our story

Rooted in faith. Sent to serve.

A Grain of Wheat Christian Ministry in Boston began with a simple vision: to see lives transformed by the love of Jesus and the power of the Gospel. We are a church family that values worship, discipleship, prayer, and faithful service.

Our mission is to help people know Christ, grow in grace, and live out their purpose in the city and beyond. We believe every person is made with intentionality and every family deserves a place of belonging.

Our values

What guides us.
